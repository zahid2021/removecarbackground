Quick answers

Who can benefit from using RemoveCarBackground?

Car dealerships, automotive photographers, and private sellers. Whether you want to streamline vehicle advert creation, reduce photography costs, or make adverts stand out — we offer scalable solutions tailored to your needs.

What devices is RemoveCarBackground available on?

Available as a web app on most computers and laptops, and as a mobile app / installable PWA for iOS and Android devices.

How does it help car dealers create professional listings?

It helps you create professional vehicle listings by replacing unwanted backgrounds with full HD car backgrounds. Listings look clean and attractive, helping you get more attention from potential buyers.

How do I get started with RemoveCarBackground?

Visit the website, sign up for a free trial, and explore the web app or mobile app for editing your car photos.

Is there a fixed-term contract?

Subscriptions are not bound to fixed-term contract periods. We only require 14-days cancellation notice.

I need help getting set up — can you help me?

Of course. Book a meeting with our support team and they’ll understand your requirements and get you set up in no time.

Can multiple users use RemoveCarBackground simultaneously?

Yes — multiple users can access accounts simultaneously and image processing is asynchronous, meaning there is no effect on processing speed.
